# Future opportunities

These ideas are intentionally outside the current launch scope.

## Online deposits and payments

Consider deposits for high-value repair intake or missed-appointment protection
only after the shop confirms refund, cancellation, and estimate policies.

## Repair-status tracking

A customer portal could show intake, estimate approval, parts ordered, bench
work, testing, and ready-for-pickup states. It would require secure repair-order
records and customer identity verification.

## Multilingual content

Spanish and other neighborhood-relevant translations could improve access.
Translate business and service copy professionally, and keep guide slugs,
canonical URLs, and structured data language-aware.

## Approved advertising

Guide pages already reserve two inert ad locations behind
`VITE_ADS_ENABLED`. Add a network only after account approval, a privacy review,
and performance testing.
